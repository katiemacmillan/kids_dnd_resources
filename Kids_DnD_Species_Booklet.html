Aasimar Handbook

Champions of the light, guided by celestial guardians!

Who are the Aasimar?

Aasimar (pronounced AH-sih-mar) are humans with a touch of celestial magic in their blood. They are born to serve as champions of good, and they often have a glowing, otherworldly beauty. Many Aasimar have sparkling eyes, metallic hair, or even a faint halo of light around them when they are feeling strong emotions!

Personality

Aasimar are usually kind, brave, and determined. From a young age, they receive dreams and visions from an angelic guide who helps them stay on the right path. Because of this, they often feel a strong sense of responsibility to help others and fight evil. However, they can sometimes be a bit too serious or hard on themselves when they make mistakes.

History and Lore

Long ago, powerful angels visited the mortal world and blessed certain families. The descendants of these families are the Aasimar. They don't have their own lands or kingdoms; instead, they live among humans, often stepping up as heroes, healers, or leaders when their communities are in danger.

General Alignment

Aasimar love doing the right thing. They are almost always Good. They believe in protecting the weak and fighting for justice, making them excellent paladins and clerics.

Feelings Towards Other Races

  • Humans: "They are our family, but sometimes they need guidance to stay out of trouble."
  • Elves: "We respect their wisdom and their love for beauty and nature."
  • Tieflings: "We might look like opposites, but we judge people by their actions, not their ancestors."

Aasimar Traits

Your Aasimar character has the following special abilities:

  • Ability Score Increase: Your Charisma score increases by 2. People naturally trust and look up to you!
  • Age: Aasimar mature at the same rate as humans but can live up to 160 years.
  • Size: Aasimar are the same size and build as humans. Your size is Medium.
  • Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
  • Darkvision: Thanks to your celestial heritage, you can see in the dark up to 60 feet as if it were dim light.
  • Celestial Resistance: You have resistance to necrotic damage and radiant damage.
  • Healing Hands: As an action, you can touch a creature and heal them! You can restore a number of hit points equal to your level. You can do this once per long rest.
  • Light Bearer: You know the Light cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it.

Your Angelic Guide

Every Aasimar has a specific celestial being (like an angel) assigned to watch over them. Think about what your guide is like! Do they speak to you in dreams? Do they send you signs, like a glowing feather or a sudden warm breeze? What is their name?

Dragonborn Handbook

Proud warriors with the blood of dragons!

Who are the Dragonborn?

Dragonborn look very much like dragons standing erect in humanoid form, though they lack wings or a tail. They are tall, strong, and covered in scaly armor that matches the color of their dragon ancestors—like shimmering gold, fiery red, or icy white. They are a proud and honorable people who value their clans above all else.

Personality

Dragonborn are known for their fierce loyalty and dedication. To a Dragonborn, honor is everything. They hate breaking promises and will always try to do their best, whether they are fighting in a battle or just helping a friend move a heavy rock. They can sometimes seem a bit serious or stubborn, but they are amazing friends to have on your side.

History and Lore

Legend says that Dragonborn were originally hatched from the eggs of the first dragons! They formed great clans and built mighty empires in ancient times. Today, they often live in close-knit communities where everyone works together and supports each other. A Dragonborn's clan is their family, and they will do anything to protect it.

General Alignment

Dragonborn love honor and rules. They tend to be Good or Lawful. They believe that a strong society needs rules to protect everyone, and they always try to be the heroes of the story.

Feelings Towards Other Races

  • Dwarves: "We respect their hard work and their loyalty to their clans. We have a lot in common!"
  • Elves: "They can be a bit flighty and unpredictable, but their magic is impressive."
  • Humans: "They are ambitious and adaptable. We enjoy working with them, as long as they keep their word."

Dragonborn Traits

Your Dragonborn character has the following special abilities:

  • Ability Score Increase: Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Charisma score increases by 1. You are strong and confident!
  • Age: Dragonborn grow quickly. They walk hours after hatching, reach the size of a 10-year-old human child by age 3, and are considered adults by 15. They live to be about 80.
  • Size: Dragonborn are taller and heavier than humans, standing well over 6 feet tall and averaging almost 250 pounds. Your size is Medium.
  • Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
  • Draconic Ancestry: You have draconic ancestry. Choose one type of dragon (like Red for fire, Blue for lightning, or White for cold). This determines your breath weapon and damage resistance.
  • Breath Weapon: You can use your action to exhale destructive energy! The type of energy matches your draconic ancestry. It's like having a built-in dragon attack!
  • Damage Resistance: You have resistance to the damage type associated with your draconic ancestry. (For example, if you are a Red Dragonborn, fire doesn't hurt you as much!)

Your Clan Name

Every Dragonborn has a personal name, but they put their clan name first as a mark of honor. A Dragonborn might introduce themselves as "Cleanthtiar Yarjerit" (Clan Cleanthtiar, name Yarjerit). What is your clan known for?

Dwarf Handbook

Stout, sturdy, and masters of stone and metal!

Who are the Dwarves?

Dwarves are short, broad, and incredibly tough. They are famous for their magnificent beards (which both men and sometimes women grow with pride!), their skill at the forge, and their deep love for the earth. They live in grand cities carved into the hearts of mountains, filled with glowing gems and echoing with the sound of hammers.

Personality

Dwarves are brave, hardworking, and very stubborn. When a Dwarf makes up their mind, it's almost impossible to change it! They value loyalty, tradition, and a job well done. They can sometimes be grumpy or suspicious of strangers, but once you earn a Dwarf's friendship, they will be your friend for life.

History and Lore

Dwarves believe they were carved from the very stone of the world by their creator god, Moradin. For thousands of years, they have mined the deep earth, fighting off goblins and dragons to protect their underground kingdoms. They keep careful records of their history and honor their ancestors by singing epic songs about their deeds.

General Alignment

Dwarves like rules and order. They are usually Lawful Good. They believe in fairness, keeping promises, and working together to build a safe and prosperous community.

Feelings Towards Other Races

  • Elves: "Too flighty and they spend too much time in the trees. But they do make beautiful things."
  • Halflings: "Good folk! They appreciate a good meal and a comfortable home, just like we do."
  • Humans: "They don't live very long, so they are always in a rush. But they are good trading partners."

Dwarf Traits

Your Dwarf character has the following special abilities:

  • Ability Score Increase: Your Constitution score increases by 2. You are incredibly tough and healthy!
  • Age: Dwarves mature at the same rate as humans, but they are considered young until they reach the age of 50. On average, they live about 350 years.
  • Size: Dwarves stand between 4 and 5 feet tall and average about 150 pounds. Your size is Medium.
  • Speed: Your base walking speed is 25 feet. Your speed is not reduced by wearing heavy armor.
  • Darkvision: Accustomed to life underground, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions up to 60 feet.
  • Dwarven Resilience: You have advantage on saving throws against poison, and you have resistance against poison damage.
  • Dwarven Combat Training: You have proficiency with the battleaxe, handaxe, light hammer, and warhammer.
  • Tool Proficiency: You gain proficiency with the artisan's tools of your choice: smith's tools, brewer's supplies, or mason's tools.
  • Stonecunning: Whenever you make an Intelligence (History) check related to the origin of stonework, you are considered proficient in the History skill and add double your proficiency bonus to the check!

Your Clan

Dwarves belong to ancient clans, like the Battlehammers or the Frostbeards. Your clan is your family and your identity. What is your clan's symbol, and what are they famous for crafting?

Elf Handbook

Graceful, magical, and at one with nature!

Who are the Elves?

Elves are a magical people of otherworldly grace, living in the world but not entirely part of it. They have pointed ears, bright eyes, and are slightly shorter and slimmer than humans. They love nature, magic, art, and music. Many Elves live in beautiful forest cities hidden among the trees or in shimmering towers of crystal.

Personality

Elves are patient, curious, and calm. Because they live for such a long time, they don't rush things. They love to explore, learn new things, and create beautiful art. They can sometimes seem a bit distant or aloof to other races, but they are deeply passionate about the things and people they love.

History and Lore

Elves are one of the oldest races in the world. They remember events that happened centuries ago as if they were yesterday. They have a deep connection to the Feywild, a magical realm of fairies and nature spirits. Because of this connection, magic flows naturally through their veins.

General Alignment

Elves love freedom and nature. They lean towards Chaotic Good. They don't like strict rules or being told what to do, but they have good hearts and want to protect the innocent and the natural world.

Feelings Towards Other Races

  • Dwarves: "They are a bit grumpy and spend too much time in dark caves, but their craftsmanship is undeniably impressive."
  • Humans: "They are so energetic and short-lived! It's fascinating to watch them, even if they are a bit reckless."
  • Halflings: "Delightful little folks. We enjoy their company and their simple joy in life."

Elf Traits

Your Elf character has the following special abilities:

  • Ability Score Increase: Your Dexterity score increases by 2. You are incredibly quick and graceful!
  • Age: Although elves reach physical maturity at about the same age as humans, the elven understanding of adulthood goes beyond physical growth to encompass worldly experience. An elf typically claims adulthood around the age of 100 and can live to be 750 years old.
  • Size: Elves range from under 5 to over 6 feet tall and have slender builds. Your size is Medium.
  • Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
  • Darkvision: Accustomed to twilit forests and the night sky, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions up to 60 feet.
  • Keen Senses: You have proficiency in the Perception skill. You notice things others miss!
  • Fey Ancestry: You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.
  • Trance: Elves don't need to sleep! Instead, they meditate deeply, remaining semiconscious, for 4 hours a day. While meditating, you can dream after a fashion; such dreams are actually mental exercises that have become reflexive through years of practice.

Your Elven Name

Elves have a child name until they declare themselves adults, at which point they choose an adult name for themselves. They also have a family name, which is often a beautiful combination of words like "Amakiir" (Gemflower) or "Galanodel" (Moonwhisper). What does your name mean?

Gnome Handbook

Curious, inventive, and full of boundless energy!

Who are the Gnomes?

Gnomes are small, cheerful folks who are bursting with enthusiasm for life. They are even shorter than dwarves, usually standing around 3 feet tall, and they often have wild hair and bright, expressive eyes. They love to invent things, play pranks, and discover how the world works. To a Gnome, every day is a new adventure!

Personality

Gnomes are incredibly curious and love to talk! They speak quickly, often jumping from one idea to the next. They are natural tinkerers and illusionists, always building strange clockwork gadgets or practicing fun magic tricks. They love jokes, games, and making people smile. It's very hard to keep a Gnome in a bad mood for long.

History and Lore

Gnomes traditionally live in hidden, underground burrows nestled under rolling hills or deep in magical forests. Their homes are cozy and filled with the sounds of whirring gears and bubbling potions. They have a special connection to the magic of the earth and often befriend burrowing animals like badgers and moles.

General Alignment

Gnomes just want to have fun and help out! They are usually Good, and often lean towards Chaotic Good. They love freedom, creativity, and making the world a happier place.

Feelings Towards Other Races

  • Dwarves: "They make great metalwork, but they need to learn how to tell a good joke!"
  • Elves: "So magical and elegant! We love sharing stories and learning about the Feywild from them."
  • Humans: "They are always busy doing something interesting. We love seeing what they'll come up with next!"

Gnome Traits

Your Gnome character has the following special abilities:

  • Ability Score Increase: Your Intelligence score increases by 2. You are super smart and quick-witted!
  • Age: Gnomes mature at the same rate humans do, and most are expected to settle down into an adult life by around age 40. They can live 350 to almost 500 years.
  • Size: Gnomes are between 3 and 4 feet tall and average about 40 pounds. Your size is Small.
  • Speed: Your base walking speed is 25 feet.
  • Darkvision: Accustomed to life underground, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions up to 60 feet.
  • Gnome Cunning: You have advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws against magic. Your mind is too quick to be easily tricked!

Your Gnome Names

Gnomes love names, and most have half a dozen or so! A gnome's mother, father, clan elder, aunts, and uncles each give the gnome a name, and various nicknames from just about everyone else might or might not stick over time. What are some of your favorite nicknames?

Goliath Handbook

Massive, incredibly strong people who live on the highest mountain peaks!

If you want to play a character who is taller than everyone else, loves a good challenge, and can lift boulders like they are pebbles, the Goliath is for you!

Personality

Goliaths are super competitive, but they also believe in playing fair. To a Goliath, life is one big game or sport! They love wrestling, racing, and seeing who can climb a mountain the fastest. They don't care much about fancy clothes or gold; they care about what you can do. If you can help the team and pull your own weight, a Goliath will be your best friend.

History & Lore

Goliaths are distant relatives of Giants! They live in nomadic tribes high up in the freezing mountains where the air is thin and the wind howls. Because life in the mountains is so tough, Goliaths learn to be strong from a very young age. They travel from peak to peak, hunting giant beasts and surviving where no one else can.

General Alignment

Most Goliaths are Lawful Neutral. This means they really care about rules, especially the rules of their tribe and the rules of a fair game. They believe everyone has a job to do, and as long as you do your job and don't cheat, you're okay in their book!

Feelings Towards Others

Goliaths judge people by how useful they are, not by how they look!

  • Dwarves: Goliaths respect Dwarves a lot because they also live in the mountains and are super tough.
  • Elves: They think Elves are a bit fragile and spend too much time reading, but they admit Elven magic is pretty cool.
  • Humans: Goliaths find Humans confusing because Humans are always changing their minds, but they respect human ambition!

Specific Traits

Giant Ancestry

You are related to Giants! You can choose a giant type (like Stone, Fire, or Cloud) to get a special magic power. For example, you might be able to turn your skin to stone to block damage, or teleport short distances like a Cloud Giant!

Powerful Build

You are super strong! Even though you are technically a "Medium" sized creature, you count as one size larger (Large) when determining how much weight you can carry, push, or drag. Need to move a wagon? No problem!

Mountain Born

You are used to the freezing cold and the high altitudes. You don't get bothered by freezing weather, and you can breathe perfectly fine at the very top of the highest mountains.

Halfling Handbook

Short, cheerful people who love good food, cozy homes, and grand adventures!

If you want to play a character who is small but mighty, loves a good joke, and has the best luck in the world, the Halfling is for you!

Personality

Halflings are the happiest people you will ever meet! They love the simple things in life: a warm fire, a good story, and lots of food (they are famous for eating second breakfast and afternoon tea!). They are incredibly brave, often standing up to monsters ten times their size just to protect their friends.

History & Lore

Halflings usually live in peaceful, hidden farming communities far away from big wars and scary monsters. They build their houses into the sides of grassy hills with round doors and windows. While most Halflings just want to stay home and farm, some get an "itch" for adventure and set off to see the world!

General Alignment

Most Halflings are Lawful Good or Neutral Good. They are kind-hearted, generous, and always want everyone to be happy. They hate seeing people get bullied and will always try to do the right thing, even if it's scary.

Feelings Towards Others

Halflings get along with almost everybody!

  • Dwarves: Halflings think Dwarves are a bit grumpy, but they share a love of good food and loyalty.
  • Elves: They think Elves are beautiful and magical, but maybe a little too serious sometimes.
  • Humans: Halflings love Humans! They make great friends and are always up for an adventure.

Specific Traits

Lucky

Halflings have magical luck! If you roll a 1 on a d20 for an attack, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die! (You must use the new roll, even if it's another 1, but that's super rare!).

Brave

Even though you are small, you have the heart of a lion! You have Advantage on saving throws against being frightened. It takes a lot to scare a Halfling!

Halfling Nimbleness

You are so small and quick that you can run right through the legs of giant monsters! You can move through the space of any creature that is a size larger than you.

Human Handbook

Adaptable, ambitious, and brave. Humans are good at a little bit of everything!

If you want to play a character who can learn any skill, fit in anywhere, and forge their own unique destiny, the Human is for you!

Personality

Humans are incredibly diverse! Some are quiet and studious, while others are loud and adventurous. Because humans don't live as long as Elves or Dwarves (who can live for hundreds of years!), humans are always in a hurry to make their mark on the world. They are curious, ambitious, and always ready to try something new.

History & Lore

Humans are the builders of empires and the explorers of the unknown. You can find them everywhere in the D&D world, from scorching deserts to freezing tundras. They build massive cities, sail across dangerous oceans, and invent new ways to use magic and technology. They are the ultimate survivors!

General Alignment

Humans can be Any Alignment! Because humans are so different from one another, they can be the greatest, most noble heroes (Lawful Good) or the sneakiest, most mischievous rogues (Chaotic Neutral). It's entirely up to you how your human behaves!

Feelings Towards Others

Humans are very social and love mixing with other cultures.

  • Dwarves: Humans respect Dwarven craftsmanship and love trading with them.
  • Elves: They admire Elven magic and art, even if they think Elves take too long to make decisions.
  • Halflings: Humans love sharing stories and meals with Halflings; they make the best travel buddies!

Specific Traits

Versatile

Because humans are so adaptable, they learn things very quickly. You get to choose one extra Skill to be Proficient in! Whether it's Acrobatics, Stealth, or History, you get to pick what makes your human special.

Resourceful

Humans always seem to find a way to succeed when things look bad. You get a free "Heroic Inspiration" every day after you finish a Long Rest. You can use this Inspiration to reroll a bad d20 roll when you really need to save the day!

Orc Handbook

Fierce, unstoppable warriors with powerful builds and tusks!

If you want to play a character who never gives up, fights with incredible passion, and protects their friends with everything they have, the Orc is for you!

Personality

Orcs are passionate and fierce! They feel their emotions very strongly—when they are happy, they laugh the loudest, and when they are angry, they roar like thunder. They are deeply loyal to their friends and family. To an Orc, actions speak louder than words. They respect bravery and honesty above all else.

History & Lore

Orcs are often misunderstood as just being scary monsters, but they actually have rich cultures and traditions! They are survivors who can live in harsh environments where others would fail, like deep canyons or wild frontiers. They honor strength and survival, and many Orcs leave their tribes to become legendary adventurers and heroes.

General Alignment

Most Orcs lean towards Chaotic Neutral or Chaotic Good. They value personal freedom and strength over strict rules and laws. They might not always follow the rules of a city, but they will always follow the rules of their heart and protect those they care about.

Feelings Towards Others

Orcs respect anyone who can hold their own in a fight or show true bravery.

  • Dwarves & Elves: Orcs might butt heads with Dwarves and Elves because of old rivalries, but if they fight side-by-side, they can become the best of friends.
  • Humans: Orcs understand Humans well because Humans are also passionate and adaptable.
  • Goliaths: Orcs and Goliaths get along great because they both love a good test of strength!

Specific Traits

Adrenaline Rush

When the battle starts, you get a burst of energy! You can run super fast toward an enemy as a Bonus Action (using the Dash action), and doing so gives you temporary extra health to protect you from hits!

Relentless Endurance

You refuse to fall down! If you get hit really hard and your Hit Points drop to 0, you can choose to drop to 1 Hit Point instead. You can do this once per Long Rest to completely surprise a monster who thought they defeated you!

Darkvision

Because Orcs often live in dark caves or hunt at night, you have excellent vision in the dark. You can see in the dark as if it were Dim Light!

Tabaxi Handbook

Cat-like adventurers who love stories, shiny treasures, and running really, really fast!

If you want to play a character with sharp claws, super sneaky skills, and endless curiosity, the Tabaxi is for you!

Personality

Tabaxi (pronounced tuh-BAHK-see) are curious, playful, and always on the move. They love collecting interesting stories, strange souvenirs, and anything that sparkles. A Tabaxi might climb a tower just to see what's on top, or chase a rumor about a lost treasure across the whole kingdom. They are friendly and funny, but they hate being bored or stuck in one place for too long!

History & Lore

Tabaxi come from warm, faraway lands filled with jungles, ruins, and ancient secrets. Their clans travel in small groups, trading tales and treasures from place to place. Many Tabaxi become rogues, rangers, or bards because they are natural explorers who love sneaking, climbing, and discovering hidden things.

General Alignment

Most Tabaxi are Chaotic Good or Chaotic Neutral. They follow their hearts, love freedom, and usually mean well — even when their curiosity gets them into trouble!

Feelings Towards Others

Tabaxi enjoy meeting new people and learning new customs.

  • Humans: Tabaxi find human cities exciting — so many shops, stories, and shiny things to see!
  • Elves: They admire Elven grace and magic, though Elves sometimes think Tabaxi move too fast.
  • Halflings: Tabaxi and Halflings often become great friends; both love food, jokes, and adventure.

Specific Traits

Darkvision

Your cat eyes see perfectly in the dark! You can see in darkness as if it were dim light for up to 60 feet.

Cat's Claws

You have sharp retractable claws. When you hit with an unarmed strike, you can deal slashing damage (1d6 + your Strength modifier) instead of normal bludgeoning damage. Scratch attack!

Cat's Talent

You are naturally sneaky and alert. You have Proficiency in Perception and Stealth — great for spotting traps and slipping past guards.

Feline Agility

When you move on your turn in combat, you can double your speed until the end of the turn. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest. Zoom!

Speed

Tabaxi are fast on their feet! Your walking speed is 40 feet instead of the usual 30.

Tiefling Handbook

People with horns and tails who have a tiny bit of fiendish magic in their blood!

If you want to play a character who looks super cool, has natural magical powers, and loves proving that you can't judge a book by its cover, the Tiefling is for you!

Personality

Tieflings are independent, mysterious, and sometimes a bit guarded. Because they have horns, tails, and sometimes brightly colored skin (like red, blue, or purple!), some people judge them before getting to know them. Because of this, Tieflings learn to take care of themselves. But once you earn a Tiefling's trust, they are fiercely loyal and will protect you forever!

History & Lore

A long, long time ago, the ancestors of the Tieflings made a deal with powerful fiends. This left a magical mark on their family line forever! Tieflings aren't evil themselves, but they carry that magical legacy. They usually live in human cities, making their own way in the world as clever rogues, brilliant wizards, or charming bards.

General Alignment

Most Tieflings are Chaotic Neutral or Chaotic Good. They don't like people telling them what to do, especially since society hasn't always been kind to them. They often want to do the right thing and prove to the world that they are heroes, not villains!

Feelings Towards Others

Tieflings are cautious around strangers, but they love making true friends.

  • Humans: Tieflings often live in Human cities, so they understand Humans well, even if Humans can be a bit judgmental.
  • Other Outcasts: Tieflings get along great with anyone who feels a bit different or left out. They appreciate anyone who looks past their appearance!

Specific Traits

Innate Magic

You have magic in your blood! Even if you aren't a Wizard or a Sorcerer, you naturally learn how to cast a few spells as you level up. You might learn how to create magical darkness to hide in, or how to rebuke enemies with a blast of fire, all without needing a spellbook!

Fiendish Legacy

Your magical blood protects you! You can choose to be resistant to Fire, Cold, or Poison damage. This means you take half damage from those attacks. Walking through fire is no big deal for a Tiefling!

Darkvision

Your magical eyes let you see perfectly well in the dark. You can see in the dark as if it were Dim Light!
