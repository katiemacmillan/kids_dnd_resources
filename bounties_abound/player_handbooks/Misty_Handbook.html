Chapter 1: How to Play

Misty, you are a hero in Bounties Abound. The Dungeon Master (DM) describes the world. You say what Misty does. Then the dice help decide what happens. You are on a team — share the adventure with your friends.

The Golden Rule

If a special rule on this book or your character sheet disagrees with a basic rule, use the special rule. Being Tiny, flying with a torn wing, and pixie magic are special rules.

The d20 Check

When you try something risky, roll a 20-sided die (d20) and add the number the DM asks for.

  1. Roll a d20.
  2. Add your bonus (like +5 for Stealth).
  3. Tell the DM the total.

If your total is equal to or higher than the Difficulty Class (DC), you succeed. Easy jobs might be DC 10. Hard jobs might be 15 or 20.

Advantage & Disadvantage

Advantage: roll two d20s and keep the higher number.

Disadvantage: roll two d20s and keep the lower number.

If you have both at once, they cancel. Just roll one d20.

Natural 20 and Natural 1

A 20 on the die is a critical success. Something extra cool happens. A 1 is a critical fail. Something goes wrong, but it can still be funny. Pixie plans going sideways is classic.

Inspiration

If you play Misty in a mischievous, brave, or clever way, the DM may give you Inspiration. Spend it later for Advantage. You can only hold one at a time.

Chapter 2: Your Turn

When a fight starts, everyone rolls Initiative: d20 + your Dexterity modifier. Misty, that is d20 + 3. Highest total goes first.

On Your Turn You Can:

  • Move 25 feet on the ground, or fly 15 feet (torn wing). You can mix walking and flying, but you cannot go farther than those speeds allow.
  • Take 1 Action (Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Hide, Help, and more).
  • Take 1 Bonus Action if a power says you can. Fey Step is a Bonus Action.
  • Talk a little. A giggle, a dare, or "watch this" is free.

You also get 1 Reaction between your turns (for example, an Opportunity Attack if a foe runs away from you).

Useful Actions

  • Attack: your dagger. This is how Sneak Attack happens.
  • Cast a Spell: Faerie Fire, Minor Illusion, Sleep, or Invisibility.
  • Hide: Stealth +5. Tiny creatures fit in teacups, curtains, and hair.
  • Help: give a friend Advantage. Buzz around a monster's face.
  • Dash / Disengage: extra move, or leave without getting stabbed.

Hitting and Missing

Roll d20 + your attack bonus. If you meet or beat the target's Armor Class (AC), you hit. Then roll damage. A natural 20 is a critical hit: roll the damage dice twice (including Sneak Attack), then add your bonus once.

Some spells use a saving throw instead. Your Spell save DC is 13.

Hit Points and Rest

You have 9 Hit Points and AC 12. You are Tiny and easy to squish if someone actually lands a hit. Hide, fly, and teleport. At 0 HP you fall unconscious and make Death Saving Throws (d20: 10+ succeeds). Three successes stabilize you. Three failures and Misty dies. A 20 wakes you with 1 HP. A 1 counts as two failures.

A Short Rest is about 1 hour. You can spend Hit Dice to heal (you have 1d8). Fey Step comes back on a rest. A Long Rest is about 8 hours. You get HP back, and your 2/day pixie-magic pool refills.

Chapter 3: Scores, Skills & Dice

Your six ability scores are who Misty is in numbers. The modifier is what you add to rolls.

STR 12 (+1) DEX 17 (+3) CON 13 (+1) INT 14 (+2) WIS 13 (+1) CHA 17 (+3)

Your Proficiency Bonus is +2. Dexterity and Charisma are your stars. You are quick, charming, and a little bit trouble.

Your Best Skills

  • Sleight of Hand +5: steal buttons, hide crumbs, palming coins.
  • Stealth +5: Tiny + sneaky is a nightmare for guards.
  • Acrobatics +5: tumbles, midair twists, landing on a hat brim.
  • Deception +4 and Performance +4: fibs, voices, putting on a show.
  • Insight +3: notice who is about to yell "hey!"

Chapter 4: You Are a Pixie

Pixies are Tiny fey: sparkle, mischief, and feelings as big as a dragon's. You are about the size of a doll. You can squeeze into places the others cannot even see. You speak Common and Sylvan (the fey language).

Size Tiny means you occupy a tiny space. You can share a friend's square (ask the DM). You might have trouble shoving big creatures, and some tools are comically huge in your hands. Your dagger is sized for you.

Torn Wing

Healthy pixies zoom. You don't. Your fly Speed is only 15 feet, and your walk Speed is 25 feet. Flying is still great for reaching shelves, windows, and chandelier chains. You just can't outrun a horse in the air. Lean into it: a lopsided flutter is part of your look.

Darkvision

You see in dim light and darkness out to 60 feet. Darkness looks like dim light to you (no color, but you can still spot shapes).

Fey Ancestry

You have Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep. Sleep spells bounce off you. (Your own Sleep spell can still knock out other people.)

Fey Step

Bonus Action, once per rest: teleport up to 30 feet to a spot you can see. Pop from a pocket to a rafter. Escape a grab. It is your best "oops, I'm caught" button.

You have no special damage resistances or vulnerabilities. Healing magic works on you (you are a living fey, not undead).

Playing Mischief

You can tease, prank, and show off, then surprise everyone by being fiercely loyal. Trouble is fun. Hurting your friends on purpose is not. Prank the villain. Help the party.

Chapter 5: You Are a Rogue

Rogues win with timing. You are a scout, a trickster, and a surprisingly sharp dagger when someone ignores the "harmless fairy."

What You Are Great At

  • Sneaking into tiny spaces.
  • Lighting enemies up with Faerie Fire so everyone can hit them.
  • Sneak Attack with your dagger.
  • Turning invisible or putting guards to sleep when you spend your daily magic.

Sneak Attack (2d6)

Once per turn, when you hit with a finesse or ranged weapon, add 2d6 extra damage if either:

  • You have Advantage on the attack, or
  • Another enemy of the target is within 5 feet of it, and you don't have Disadvantage.

Your dagger is a finesse weapon. A friend standing next to the monster is enough. Hidden + Advantage is even better. On a critical hit, roll those Sneak Attack dice twice too.

(Most level 1 rogues only get 1d6. You get 2d6. That's your special pixie-rogue punch.)

Coming at Level 2: Cunning Action

You are level 1, so Dash, Disengage, and Hide still cost your Action. At level 2, rogues usually get Cunning Action: do one of those as a Bonus Action. Until then, Fey Step is your Bonus Action escape hatch.

Growing Stronger

As you level up (usually through level 5), rogues typically gain Cunning Action, a subclass, more Sneak Attack, and later Uncanny Dodge. The DM will tell you the exact features. Your pixie magic stays part of who you are.

Chapter 6: Your Tricks & Magic

Pixie magic uses Charisma. Spell save DC is 13.

At Will (no limit)

Faerie Fire. Action. Concentration, up to 1 minute. Each object in a 20-foot cube, and each creature who fails a Dexterity save (DC 13), lights up with sparkles. Attack rolls against glowing creatures have Advantage. They can't turn Invisible. This is how you set up Sneak Attack for you and your friends.

Minor Illusion. Action. Create a sound or a still image (5-foot cube) for 1 minute. A fake mouse, a giggle in the hall, a tiny "closed" sign. Physical interaction reveals it's fake. A creature can investigate (Intelligence check DC 13) to see through it.

Shared Pool (2 per day)

You can cast Sleep or Invisibility a combined 2 times per day. That's two Sleeps, two Invisibilities, or one of each. They come back after a Long Rest.

Sleep. Action. Pick a point within 90 feet. 5d8 hit points worth of creatures in a 20-foot radius fall unconscious, starting with the lowest current HP (skip creatures that are undead or immune to being charmed). A slap or damage wakes them.

Invisibility. Action. You or a creature you touch becomes Invisible for up to 1 hour (concentration). Anything they are wearing or carrying is invisible too. The spell ends early if the target attacks or casts a spell. Great for scouting. Remember: attacking ends it, so stab after you appear, or accept that the spell pops.

Weapon

Dagger. Attack +5. Hit: 1d4 + 3 piercing. Add 2d6 Sneak Attack when you qualify.

A Simple Combat Plan

  1. Faerie Fire the scary monster (Advantage for the whole team).
  2. Hide or get a friend next to it, then dagger + 2d6.
  3. If you get grabbed, Bonus Action Fey Step 30 feet away.
  4. Save Sleep and Invisibility for "we really need this."

Chapter 7: Quick Reminders

How to Be a Great Player

D&D is a team game. You are not trying to beat the DM. You are telling a story with your friends.

  • Share the spotlight. Plan heists together. Let someone else be the doctor, the blaster, or the joke-teller. Then you cause trouble.
  • Mischief with kindness. Pranks should make the table laugh, not make a friend feel picked on.
  • Listen to the DM. Tiny details (keyholes, cake crumbs, open windows) are your whole world.
  • Accept failure. Illusions that fail are still funny.
  • Be creative. You can ride on a hat, steal a monocle, or tie shoelaces together. Ask "what would a pixie try?"
