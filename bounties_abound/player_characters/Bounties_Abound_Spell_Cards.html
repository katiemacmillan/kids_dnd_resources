Bounties Abound Spell Cards

Wren’s 9 cards, Sanebs’s 3 cards, then Misty’s 4 cards. Each face page is followed by a matching back page.

  1. Print on Letter paper, portrait.
  2. Turn on Background graphics.
  3. Choose two-sided / duplex and flip on the long edge (not the short edge).
  4. Cut along the gold borders. Wren = mossy green, Sanebs = slate blue, Misty = pink.

Wren — fronts (page 1 of 2)

Wren

Spare the Dying

Cantrip • Necromancy
Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Duration: Instantaneous
Touch a living creature at 0 HP. They become stable (they stop dying). Does not work on undead or constructs — it will not help Sanebs!
Wren

Mending

Cantrip • Transmutation
Time: 1 minute
Range: Touch
Duration: Instantaneous
Fix a small break or tear (broken key, torn cloak, cracked cup) no longer than 1 foot. Leaves no scar. Cannot put the magic back into a magic item.
Wren

Mage Hand

Cantrip • Conjuration
Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Duration: 1 minute
A floating spectral hand appears. Use your action to pick up light objects (up to 10 pounds), open an unlocked door, or press a button. It cannot attack, and it vanishes if it goes more than 30 feet from you.
Wren

Cure Wounds

Level 1 • Evocation • uses a slot
Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Duration: Instantaneous
Touch a living creature. They regain 1d8+3 hit points. No effect on undead or constructs.
Wren

Inflict Wounds

Level 1 • Necromancy • uses a slot
Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Duration: Instantaneous
A nasty plague-doctor poke. Make a melee spell attack +5. On a hit, the target takes 3d10 necrotic damage. You have to get close!
Wren

Sanctuary

Level 1 • Abjuration • uses a slot
Time: 1 bonus action
Range: 30 feet
Duration: 1 minute
Ward a friend. A monster that tries to attack them must pass a Wisdom save (DC 13) or pick someone else. Does not stop area effects. Ends if the warded creature attacks.
Wren

Entangle

Level 1 • Conjuration • uses a slot
Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Vines fill a 20-foot square. The ground is hard to walk through. Creatures there make a Strength save (DC 13) or are restrained. A restrained creature can use an action to try to break free (Strength check DC 13).
Wren

Comprehend Languages

Level 1 • Divination • uses a slot
Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Duration: 1 hour
You understand any spoken language you hear. You can also read writing by touching it (about 1 minute per page). Does not decode secret codes or magical glyphs.
Wren

Detect Poison and Disease

Level 1 • Divination • uses a slot
Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
Sense poisons, poisonous creatures, and diseases within 30 feet, and identify what they are. Perfect plague-doctor work.

Sanebs — fronts (page 1 of 2)

Sanebs

Eldritch Blast

Cantrip • Evocation
Time: 1 action
Range: 120 feet
Duration: Instantaneous
A beam of crackling energy. Ranged spell attack +5. Hit: 1d10 force damage. You can cast this all day! At 5th level you fire two beams.
Sanebs

Fire Bolt

Cantrip • Evocation
Time: 1 action
Range: 120 feet
Duration: Instantaneous
Hurl a mote of fire. Ranged spell attack +5. Hit: 1d10 fire damage. Can light a torch, rope, or other unattended object on fire.
Sanebs

Detect Evil and Good

Level 1 • Divination • uses a slot
Time: 1 action
Range: Self
Duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes
You know if angels, fiends, fey, elementals, or undead are within 30 feet, and where they are. Also senses holy or unholy places nearby.

Misty — fronts (page 1 of 2)

Misty

Faerie Fire

At will • Evocation
Time: 1 action
Range: 60 feet
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute
Sparkly light fills a 20-foot cube. Creatures there make a Dexterity save (DC 13) or glow. Attacks against glowing creatures have Advantage, and they can’t hide.
Misty

Minor Illusion

At will • Illusion
Time: 1 action
Range: 30 feet
Duration: 1 minute
Create a sound or a small fake image (up to a 5-foot cube). Great for pranks! A creature can inspect it (Investigation vs DC 13) to see through the trick.
Misty

Sleep

2/day pool • Enchantment
Time: 1 action
Range: 90 feet
Duration: 1 minute
Roll 5d8. That many HP of creatures in a 20-foot area fall asleep, starting with the weakest. Undead don’t sleep. Uses one of your 2 daily pixie-magic uses.
Misty

Invisibility

2/day pool • Illusion
Time: 1 action
Range: Touch
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 hour
You or a friend you touch turn invisible (gear too). Ends if that person attacks or casts a spell. Uses one of your 2 daily pixie-magic uses.
