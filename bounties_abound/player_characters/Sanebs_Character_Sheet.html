What I Look Like

A short, lazy-looking skeleton with a wide grin and glowing pinprick eyes. Sanebs wears a baggy blue jacket over adventuring clothes, hands always in the pockets. A faded judge's sash hides under the hoodie, and a tiny wooden gavel lives in a pocket. Tiny bones, big attitude — used to decide cases. Still does, sort of.

Resistances & Vulnerabilities

Vulnerable — Bludgeoning

Clubs, hammers, rocks, and falling hurt you extra (double damage). Bones and blunt hits do not mix!

Immune — Poison

Poison damage does nothing. You cannot be poisoned. You also do not get exhausted the way living people do.

Undead note: Spells like Cure Wounds and Spare the Dying usually do not work on you. Resting still helps — ask the DM.

Detect Evil and Good
Action · Self · up to 10 minutes · uses your 1st-level slot

You sense whether angels, fiends, fey, elementals, or undead are within 30 feet, and where they are. Great for spotting spooky secrets.

Special Abilities

Lucky. If you roll a 1 on a d20 for an attack, check, or save, reroll it. You must use the new roll.
Nimble Escape. Bonus Action: Disengage (walk away safely) or Hide. Shortcut out of trouble!
Relentless Endurance (1/long rest). If you drop to 0 HP, you can drop to 1 HP instead. Surprise — still standing.
The Last Word (Judge). In a dispute about a crime or a lie, people often let you speak last — and they listen. You can get a short meeting with a mayor or guard captain.
Pact Magic. You only have one big spell slot, but it returns after a short rest. Cantrips never run out.

On Your Turn

  • Move up to 25 feet (you can slip through bigger creatures’ spaces — you’re Small).
  • Action: usually Eldritch Blast or Fire Bolt. Short sword if someone is right next to you.
  • Bonus Action: Nimble Escape (Disengage or Hide) if you need it.
  • Watch out for hammers and clubs — bludgeoning hurts twice as much!
