Special Abilities

Sneak Attack (1d6). Once per turn, when you hit with a finesse or ranged weapon, add +1d6 damage if you have Advantage, or if a friend is standing next to that monster (and you don’t have Disadvantage).
Feline Agility (1/rest). Double your speed until the end of this turn (80 feet!). Zoom!
Cat’s Claws. Scratch for 1d6+3 slashing. You are also a great climber — ask to scale walls, wagons, and fancy furniture.
Darkvision. Cat eyes! See in darkness out to 60 feet as if it were dim light.

How to Sneak Attack

  • Hide, then attack (Advantage) — extra 1d6!
  • Attack a monster that is already next to Sanebs or Wren — extra 1d6!
  • You only get the extra dice once per turn, even if you hit twice.
  • Never stand in the middle of the room. Stab, then get behind a barrel.

On Your Turn

  • Move up to 40 feet (80 if you pop Feline Agility).
  • Action: Short sword or dagger. Try to Sneak Attack every round.
  • You are the party’s lockpicker, pocket-picker, and button-presser. Steal the weird item.
