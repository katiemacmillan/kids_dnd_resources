Cantrips — cast anytime

Spare the Dying
Action · Touch

Touch a living friend at 0 HP. They become stable (they stop dying). Does not work on undead (sorry, Sanebs!).

Mending
1 minute · Touch

Repair a small break or tear (broken key, torn cloak, cracked cup). Super useful between fights.

Mage Hand
Action · 30 feet · 1 minute

A floating spectral hand picks up light objects, opens unlocked doors, or presses buttons from a safe distance. Perfect plague-doctor helper.

1st-Level Spells in My Book

Cure Wounds
Action · Touch

Heal a living creature you touch for 1d8+3 HP. No effect on undead.

Inflict Wounds
Action · Touch · attack +5

A nasty plague-doctor poke. 3d10 necrotic on a hit. You have to get close!

Sanctuary
Bonus action · 30 feet · 1 minute

Ward a friend. An enemy must pass a Wisdom save (DC 13) to attack them, or pick someone else.

Entangle
Action · 90 feet · up to 1 minute

Vines fill a 20-foot square. Creatures there make a Strength save (DC 13) or are restrained.

Comprehend Languages
Action · Self · 1 hour

Understand any spoken language you hear, and written words you touch.

Detect Poison and Disease
Action · Self · up to 10 minutes

Sense poisons, poisonous creatures, and diseases within 30 feet — and identify what they are.
