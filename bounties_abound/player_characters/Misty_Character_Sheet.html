Special Abilities

Sneak Attack (2d6). Once per turn, when you hit with a finesse or ranged weapon, add +2d6 if you have Advantage, or if a friend is within 5 feet of that monster (and you don’t have Disadvantage).
Fey Step (1/rest). Bonus Action: teleport up to 30 feet to an empty space you can see. Pop!
Darkvision. See in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright, and in darkness as if it were dim light.
Fey Ancestry. Advantage on saves against being charmed. Magic can’t put you to sleep (Sleep the spell still skips you!).

Pixie Magic

Faerie Fire
At will · Action · 60 feet · Concentration, 1 minute

Sparkly outline in a 20-foot cube. Dexterity save DC 13 or they glow. Attacks against glowing creatures have Advantage, and they can’t hide.

Minor Illusion
At will · Action · 30 feet · 1 minute

Create a sound or a small fake image (a 5-foot cube). Perfect for pranks. A creature can inspect it (Investigation vs DC 13) to see through it.

Sleep
2/day pool · Action · 90 feet · 1 minute

Roll 5d8. That many HP of creatures in a 20-foot area nod off, starting with the weakest. Undead don’t sleep.

Invisibility
2/day pool · Action · Touch · Concentration, up to 1 hour

You or a friend you touch turn invisible. Clothes and stuff you’re holding vanish too. Ends if that person attacks or casts a spell.

On Your Turn

  • Move 25 feet on foot, or fly 15 feet (torn wing — short hops).
  • Action: Dagger, Faerie Fire, Minor Illusion, Sleep, or Invisibility.
  • Bonus Action: Fey Step (teleport 30 feet) if you still have it.
  • Hide, then stab for Sneak Attack — or glow a monster with Faerie Fire so friends get Advantage!
