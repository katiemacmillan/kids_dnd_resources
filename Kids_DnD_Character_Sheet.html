How to Use This Sheet

  • When the DM says “roll [skill]” → find it below, roll d20 + your number.
  • When you attack → roll d20 + To Hit. If you beat the monster’s AC, roll Damage.
  • On your turn you get: Move + Action + maybe a Bonus Action (only if a spell or ability says so).
  • At 0 HP you fall unconscious — friends can heal you!

Quick Reminders

  • Advantage = roll 2d20, use the higher number.
  • Disadvantage = roll 2d20, use the lower number.
  • Natural 20 on an attack = automatic hit + extra damage dice!
  • Natural 1 on an attack = automatic miss.
  • Short rest (1 hour) = catch your breath, spend hit dice to heal.
  • Long rest (8 hours sleep) = full HP & all spell slots back.
