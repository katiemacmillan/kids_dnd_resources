# Welcome to Dungeons & Dragons! 🐉⚔️

Dungeons & Dragons (D&D) is a game of imagination, storytelling, and rolling dice! You and your friends will create heroes, explore dangerous dungeons, fight monsters, and find epic treasure. 

The **Dungeon Master (DM)** (that's your game runner!) will describe the world, and you will decide what your character does. 

---

## 🎲 The Golden Rule: The d20
Whenever you want to do something risky—like swinging a sword at a goblin, jumping over a pit, or sneaking past a sleeping dragon—you roll the **20-sided die (d20)**. 

Here is how it works:
1. **Roll the d20.**
2. **Add your bonus** (written on your character sheet).
3. **Tell the DM your total.** 

If your total meets or beats the Target Number (the DM knows this number in secret), you succeed! If it’s lower, you fail (which usually leads to something funny or dangerous!).

## 🦸‍♂️ Creating Your Character

To make your hero, you will choose three main things:

### 1. Your Race (Who you are)
Your race gives you special traits and determines what you look like.
* **Humans:** Brave and good at a little bit of everything.
* **Elves:** Graceful, magical, and have pointy ears. They see well in the dark!
* **Dwarves:** Tough, bearded warriors who love mining and mountains.
* **Halflings:** Short, lucky, and very sneaky. 
* **Dragonborn:** Tall, scaly dragon-people who can breathe fire, ice, or lightning!
* *(There are many more, like Tieflings, Orcs, and Gnomes!)*

### 2. Your Class (What you do)
Your class is your job in the adventuring party. *(Note: You will get a separate mini-book for your specific class later!)*
* **Fighters & Barbarians:** The tough warriors who hit things hard.
* **Rogues:** The sneaky ninjas who pick locks and disarm traps.
* **Wizards & Sorcerers:** The magic users who cast powerful spells.
* **Clerics & Paladins:** The holy healers and protectors.
* **Rangers & Druids:** The nature lovers with animal friends.

### 3. Your Ability Scores (Your Stats)
Every character has 6 main stats that measure how good they are at different things. 
* 💪 **Strength (STR):** How much you can lift and how hard you hit with big weapons.
* 🏃 **Dexterity (DEX):** How fast you are, how well you dodge, and how good you are with a bow.
* 🛡️ **Constitution (CON):** How tough you are and how much health you have.
* 🧠 **Intelligence (INT):** How book-smart you are (knowing history, magic, and science).
* 🦉 **Wisdom (WIS):** How street-smart you are (noticing hidden things, surviving in the wild).
* ✨ **Charisma (CHA):** How charming, funny, and persuasive you are.

## ⚔️ How to Play: Combat!
When monsters attack, everybody rolls **Initiative** (a d20 + your DEX bonus) to see who goes first. 

When it is your turn, you can do **THREE** things:

1. **Move:** You can walk up to your speed (usually 30 feet, or 6 squares on a map).
2. **Action:** This is your main move! You can:
   * **Attack:** Swing your sword or shoot your bow.
   * **Cast a Spell:** Use magic.
   * **Dash:** Run twice as fast.
   * **Dodge:** Focus on dodging (monsters will have a hard time hitting you).
   * **Help:** Give a friend an advantage on their next roll.
3. **Bonus Action:** A quick extra move. *You can only do this if a spell or class rule specifically says it takes a "Bonus Action."*

### Health & Healing
* **Hit Points (HP):** This is your health. If you take damage, your HP goes down. 
* **Zero HP:** If your HP hits 0, you fall unconscious. You aren't dead yet, but your friends need to heal you quickly!
* **Resting:** You can take a **Short Rest** (a 1-hour break to eat and bandage up) to get some HP back, or a **Long Rest** (8 hours of sleep) to heal completely and get all your magic back.

## ✨ Magic Basics
If your character can cast spells, you have two types of magic:

* **Cantrips:** Simple, easy magic tricks. You can cast these as many times as you want, all day long!
* **Leveled Spells:** Powerful magic. To cast these, you have to spend a **Spell Slot** (think of them like magic energy batteries). 
  * If you cast a Level 1 spell, you use up a Level 1 Spell Slot. 
  * When you run out of Spell Slots, you can't cast powerful magic until you take a Long Rest to recharge!

**Spell Attacks vs. Saving Throws:**
Sometimes *you* roll a d20 to see if your spell hits the monster. Other times, the spell just happens, and the *monster* has to roll a d20 (a Saving Throw) to try and dodge out of the way!

## 🎒 Gear & Equipment Basics
You will carry a backpack full of adventuring gear like rope, torches, and rations (food). But the most important gear is for fighting:

* **Armor Class (AC):** This is the target number monsters have to roll to hit you. Better armor = higher AC! If your AC is 15, a goblin has to roll a 15 or higher to hurt you.
* **Melee Weapons:** Swords, axes, and daggers. You use these when standing right next to a monster. (Usually uses Strength).
* **Ranged Weapons:** Bows and crossbows. You use these to shoot from far away! (Usually uses Dexterity).
* **Damage Dice:** If your attack hits, you roll a different die (like a d6 or d8) to see how much damage you do. Bigger weapons use bigger dice!
