Aasimar Handbook

Champions of the light, guided by celestial guardians!

Who are the Aasimar?

Aasimar (pronounced AH-sih-mar) are humans with a touch of celestial magic in their blood. They are born to serve as champions of good, and they often have a glowing, otherworldly beauty. Many Aasimar have sparkling eyes, metallic hair, or even a faint halo of light around them when they are feeling strong emotions!

Personality

Aasimar are usually kind, brave, and determined. From a young age, they receive dreams and visions from an angelic guide who helps them stay on the right path. Because of this, they often feel a strong sense of responsibility to help others and fight evil. However, they can sometimes be a bit too serious or hard on themselves when they make mistakes.

History and Lore

Long ago, powerful angels visited the mortal world and blessed certain families. The descendants of these families are the Aasimar. They don't have their own lands or kingdoms; instead, they live among humans, often stepping up as heroes, healers, or leaders when their communities are in danger.

General Alignment

Aasimar love doing the right thing. They are almost always Good. They believe in protecting the weak and fighting for justice, making them excellent paladins and clerics.

Feelings Towards Other Races

  • Humans: "They are our family, but sometimes they need guidance to stay out of trouble."
  • Elves: "We respect their wisdom and their love for beauty and nature."
  • Tieflings: "We might look like opposites, but we judge people by their actions, not their ancestors."

Aasimar Traits

Your Aasimar character has the following special abilities:

  • Ability Score Increase: Your Charisma score increases by 2. People naturally trust and look up to you!
  • Age: Aasimar mature at the same rate as humans but can live up to 160 years.
  • Size: Aasimar are the same size and build as humans. Your size is Medium.
  • Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
  • Darkvision: Thanks to your celestial heritage, you can see in the dark up to 60 feet as if it were dim light.
  • Celestial Resistance: You have resistance to necrotic damage and radiant damage.
  • Healing Hands: As an action, you can touch a creature and heal them! You can restore a number of hit points equal to your level. You can do this once per long rest.
  • Light Bearer: You know the Light cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it.

Your Angelic Guide

Every Aasimar has a specific celestial being (like an angel) assigned to watch over them. Think about what your guide is like! Do they speak to you in dreams? Do they send you signs, like a glowing feather or a sudden warm breeze? What is their name?
