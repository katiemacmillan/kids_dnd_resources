Elf Handbook

Graceful, magical, and at one with nature!

Who are the Elves?

Elves are a magical people of otherworldly grace, living in the world but not entirely part of it. They have pointed ears, bright eyes, and are slightly shorter and slimmer than humans. They love nature, magic, art, and music. Many Elves live in beautiful forest cities hidden among the trees or in shimmering towers of crystal.

Personality

Elves are patient, curious, and calm. Because they live for such a long time, they don't rush things. They love to explore, learn new things, and create beautiful art. They can sometimes seem a bit distant or aloof to other races, but they are deeply passionate about the things and people they love.

History and Lore

Elves are one of the oldest races in the world. They remember events that happened centuries ago as if they were yesterday. They have a deep connection to the Feywild, a magical realm of fairies and nature spirits. Because of this connection, magic flows naturally through their veins.

General Alignment

Elves love freedom and nature. They lean towards Chaotic Good. They don't like strict rules or being told what to do, but they have good hearts and want to protect the innocent and the natural world.

Feelings Towards Other Races

  • Dwarves: "They are a bit grumpy and spend too much time in dark caves, but their craftsmanship is undeniably impressive."
  • Humans: "They are so energetic and short-lived! It's fascinating to watch them, even if they are a bit reckless."
  • Halflings: "Delightful little folks. We enjoy their company and their simple joy in life."

Elf Traits

Your Elf character has the following special abilities:

  • Ability Score Increase: Your Dexterity score increases by 2. You are incredibly quick and graceful!
  • Age: Although elves reach physical maturity at about the same age as humans, the elven understanding of adulthood goes beyond physical growth to encompass worldly experience. An elf typically claims adulthood around the age of 100 and can live to be 750 years old.
  • Size: Elves range from under 5 to over 6 feet tall and have slender builds. Your size is Medium.
  • Speed: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
  • Darkvision: Accustomed to twilit forests and the night sky, you have superior vision in dark and dim conditions up to 60 feet.
  • Keen Senses: You have proficiency in the Perception skill. You notice things others miss!
  • Fey Ancestry: You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.
  • Trance: Elves don't need to sleep! Instead, they meditate deeply, remaining semiconscious, for 4 hours a day. While meditating, you can dream after a fashion; such dreams are actually mental exercises that have become reflexive through years of practice.

Your Elven Name

Elves have a child name until they declare themselves adults, at which point they choose an adult name for themselves. They also have a family name, which is often a beautiful combination of words like "Amakiir" (Gemflower) or "Galanodel" (Moonwhisper). What does your name mean?
