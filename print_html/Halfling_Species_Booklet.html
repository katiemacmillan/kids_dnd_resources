Halfling Species Handbook

Halfling Handbook

Short, cheerful people who love good food, cozy homes, and grand adventures!

If you want to play a character who is small but mighty, loves a good joke, and has the best luck in the world, the Halfling is for you!

Personality

Halflings are the happiest people you will ever meet! They love the simple things in life: a warm fire, a good story, and lots of food (they are famous for eating second breakfast and afternoon tea!). They are incredibly brave, often standing up to monsters ten times their size just to protect their friends.

History & Lore

Halflings usually live in peaceful, hidden farming communities far away from big wars and scary monsters. They build their houses into the sides of grassy hills with round doors and windows. While most Halflings just want to stay home and farm, some get an "itch" for adventure and set off to see the world!

General Alignment

Most Halflings are Lawful Good or Neutral Good. They are kind-hearted, generous, and always want everyone to be happy. They hate seeing people get bullied and will always try to do the right thing, even if it's scary.

Feelings Towards Others

Halflings get along with almost everybody!

  • Dwarves: Halflings think Dwarves are a bit grumpy, but they share a love of good food and loyalty.
  • Elves: They think Elves are beautiful and magical, but maybe a little too serious sometimes.
  • Humans: Halflings love Humans! They make great friends and are always up for an adventure.

Specific Traits

Lucky

Halflings have magical luck! If you roll a 1 on a d20 for an attack, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die! (You must use the new roll, even if it's another 1, but that's super rare!).

Brave

Even though you are small, you have the heart of a lion! You have Advantage on saving throws against being frightened. It takes a lot to scare a Halfling!

Halfling Nimbleness

You are so small and quick that you can run right through the legs of giant monsters! You can move through the space of any creature that is a size larger than you.
